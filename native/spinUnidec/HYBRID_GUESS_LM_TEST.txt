2D FIT experimental hybrid optimiser
====================================

This build tests a two-stage fitting strategy:

1. Run the historical restrained guess-and-check optimiser to completion.
2. Use that fitted solution (including line shapes and least-squares amplitudes)
   as the starting point for the continuous LM optimiser.
3. During LM, each peak centre is restricted to +/- half of the extraction
   radius around its position at the end of stage 1.
4. Existing constraints remain in force: positive Gaussian/Lorentzian widths
   and 0 <= voigt <= 1.
5. LM is accepted only if its finite SSE improves on the guess-and-check SSE
   by more than a tiny numerical tolerance. Otherwise the stage-1 result is
   retained unchanged.
6. A diagnostic line is printed for every fit group, e.g.:
     FIT group 14: Guess SSE=... LM SSE=... LM accepted
   or
     FIT group 39: Guess SSE=... LM SSE=... LM rejected

No changes were made to the peak model, ROI construction, FitRad/FitF1/FitF2
selection logic, or FUDA-style output format.

The earlier LM-test correction that includes cross-peak terms in J^T J for
overlap groups is retained.
